Ageing · Loneliness · Animation · Film

The Steamboat Willie Story

A film for Retiro dos Artistas, the Brazilian retirement home for actors and artists: the world’s most famous 96-year-old — and recently retired — spokesmouse, used to put a spotlight on the loneliness and challenges of ageing performers.

Client
Retiro dos Artistas
Agency
Klick Health
Year
2024

The idea

When Steamboat Willie entered the public domain in 2024, the character became a 96-year-old retiree — and a way to tell the story of the real retired artists Retiro dos Artistas cares for. Klick Health made the film with Lightfarm Studios and Jamute.

The result

LIA 2024: Gold for Direction and Gold for Stop-Frame Animation, Silver for Direct Film, Finalist for Sound Design. Clio Health 2025: Gold, Design Craft (Animation) and Bronze, Film Craft (Animation). Also honoured at the ADC 104th Annual Awards.

Awards

  • London International Awards 2024 — Gold, Direction; Gold, Stop-Frame Animation; Silver, Direct Film; Finalist, Sound Design
  • Clio Health 2025 — Gold, Design Craft: Animation; Bronze, Film Craft: Animation
