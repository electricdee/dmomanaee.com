Keytruda & Keytruda QLEX

Two years of 360° work for the world’s most prescribed cancer immunotherapy — keytruda.com, keytrudahcp.com, national TV, and the digital campaign — plus the launch of Keytruda QLEX, a new subcutaneous route of delivery.

Client
Merck
Agency
Digitas Health
Role
Creative Director
Year
June 2024–2026

The challenge

Bringing access to more patients with a subcutaneous administration starts with letting them and their caretakers know there is something new happening with life-saving Keytruda.

The idea

Multiple rounds of message testing, concept testing and creative execution yielded a DTC website that connects patients to a life-saving treatment they can receive in one minute. HCP campaign and messaging for Merck Keytruda, and their newest subcutaneous product Keytruda QLEX.

The result

Fully integrated campaigns across TV, digital, display, website, HCP detail aids, conference materials, and a new route-of-delivery launch. Retained and grew the world’s largest DTC oncology account by expanding into HCP.
