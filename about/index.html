Who is Darian Momanaee?

Darian Momanaee is a creative director in advertising, known for multi-award winning campaigns “The Most Beautiful Sound,” “The Steamboat Willie Story,” brand launches and TV commercials (Lilly, Merck, Mercedes-Benz, LG), Webby-recognized websites, and as a juror and mentor for esteemed organizations such as D&AD and Clios Health.

With almost 20 years of experience helping make his clients famous across everything from mass-market electronics to rare disease pharmaceutical treatments, Darian’s work is known for using surprising scientific, medical and product insights to tell deeply emotional stories that move people. His passion and experience come to life in oncology, rare disease, gastroenterology, patient services, women’s health, pediatrics, ophthalmology, neurology, mental health, social isolation & loneliness, hearing science, electronics, new technology, cultural understanding, underserved communities, and AI.

In school, Darian studied music and literature (Vanderbilt University, The University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, The University of Texas at Austin) and recently won back-to-back Gold Clios for sound design (2023 for GREY, 2024 for Klick Health). He currently lives in Harlem, New York City.
